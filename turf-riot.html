Everyone else on the field is a bot. Standing in your spawn area heals you.

Loot

Hearts refill your health. Armour plates add 20% armour, x2 power doubles your damage and fastest boots double your speed. The freeze gun replaces your weapon for 3 shots, and each hit freezes an enemy for 3 seconds.

You keep loot until you go down, then it drops where you fell for anyone to grab. A used-up freeze gun turns up somewhere new a minute later.
